      How to Play

      Left Rim (Ka)D  or  ←
      Drum Center (Don)F  or  ↓
      Right Rim (Ka)J  or  →

      Notes fall toward the drum. Strike the matching lane as the note crosses the hit line. Perfect ≤50ms · Good ≤110ms.

      Esc quits a song or test-play.
